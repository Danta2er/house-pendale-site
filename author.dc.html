The Author

Liora Pendale

Hello, darlings. I write erotic urban fantasy because I don't think craving needs an apology, and I don't think a love scene is where the writing gets to relax. Every scene I put my name on has to be doing the same work as the plot around it: building something, costing something, changing someone.

I write structure with teeth. Magic that has rules. Submission that has weight. Orgasms with consequences attached. If it reads as lush, good, that's the point. If it reads as careless, tell me, because that's the one thing I won't forgive myself for.

People ask what I studied before this. Nothing useful. Fiction taught me everything I know about pacing. It also taught me a few things about stamina no professor ever covered.

~ L.
