# Magic Systems Page Contract

## Identity

- Page name: Magic Systems
- Slug / route: `/magic-systems/`
- Page family: Collection
- Status: Seeded v1

## Page job

- Primary purpose: Publicly orient readers to the three foundational magical systems of the Pendaleverse.
- Secondary purpose: Show that the world has coherent rules without turning the site into a dissertation bunker.
- Conversion target: Move readers toward system detail pages, the books, and deeper optional notes where appropriate.
- Success signal: A visitor can understand the names and distinct functions of `Pulse Resonance`, `Wordframe`, and `Covenant`, then choose where to go next.

## Audience

- Primary audience: Readers intrigued by world logic who want enough structure to trust the cosmology.
- Entry state: Curious, slightly cautious, often worried that fantasy systems will become incomprehensible sludge.
- What they probably already know: The world feels ritualized and rule-bound.
- What they do not know yet: The actual foundational system names and how they differ.

## Emotional job

- Desired emotional effect: Confidence, intrigue, and respect for the intelligence of the system.
- Tension to create: The feeling that the world's desire and danger obey grammar.
- Relief or reward to provide: A clear map of the core systems without punishing the reader for being curious.
- What the page must never feel like: A lore wiki, a metaphysics lecture, or a TED Talk in a velvet collar.

## Content boundaries

- Must include: `Pulse Resonance`, `Wordframe`, and `Covenant` as the only top-level foundational systems.
- Must imply: These systems create downstream consequences and practices, but not every consequence needs to be canon-pinned on the public site.
- Must not include: `Mirror` as an active foundational system or `Constellation Bonding` as a named architecture block.
- Must stay offsite: Deep essays, intimate implications, evolving edge theory, and anything explicit or over-defined that belongs on Substack.

## Narrative and brand rules

- Brand posture: Clear, exacting, and a little dangerous.
- Voice notes: Explain cleanly without stripping the system of mystery.
- Explicitness boundary: Clinical lore voice is wrong; explicit demonstration is also wrong. Stay charged but controlled.
- Lore boundary: Foundation only. Leave room for creative movement.

## Information architecture role

- Upstream entry points: Home, Books, Character pages, search.
- Downstream destinations: Individual system pages for `Pulse Resonance`, `Wordframe`, `Covenant`, and an optional secondary page such as `How Collaring Works`.
- Related pages: Books, `When Stars Align`, future House and Journal pages.

## CTA logic

- Primary CTA: Explore the systems
- Secondary CTA: Return to the books
- Tertiary CTA: Read deeper notes on Substack

## Social and channel routing

- Social presence on this page: Minimal support only.
- Substack role on this page: Main outlet for deeper system essays and evolving interpretations.
- Amazon or retailer role on this page: Secondary book bridge only.

## Asset and content needs

- Required copy blocks: Collection intro, three system summaries, optional downstream-practice note, related-book bridge.
- Required media: Optional symbolic or atmospheric support; avoid turning systems into clip-art tarot homework.
- Optional enhancements: Diagrammatic relationships, glossary teaser, secondary callout for `How Collaring Works`.

## SEO and metadata intent

- Working title: Magic Systems | House Pendale
- Working meta description: Learn the foundational magical systems of House Pendale: Pulse Resonance, Wordframe, and Covenant.
- Structured data notes: `CollectionPage`; individual system entries should handle their own richer metadata later.

## Tech handoff notes

- Template type: Collection template with scalable system cards and route-out to detail pages.
- Reusable components likely needed: Systems hero, system cards, related-book bridge, optional downstream-practice callout.
- Future expansion hooks: Individual system pages, companion essays, glossary support, gated expansions if ever needed.

## Creative handoff notes

- Core promise: The world has rules, and those rules are part of what makes it seductive.
- Key beats: Orientation -> three foundational systems -> downstream consequence hint -> route back to books or deeper notes.
- Phrases or imagery to lean toward: grammar, current, vow, structure, resonance, words that change the air, consequences with clean edges.
- Phrases or imagery to avoid: fake-academic bloat, endless taxonomy, deprecated system names, claims that pin down fluid canon too early.
