# Magic Systems Creative Brief

## Page

- Page name: Magic Systems
- Based on contract: `docs/briefs/pages/magic-systems/page-contract.md`

## Creative objective

- What this page needs to make the reader feel: That the magic is coherent, charged, and worth trusting.
- What this page needs the reader to do next: Choose a system to learn more about, or return to the books with stronger curiosity.

## Audience and posture

- Reader type: Worldbuilding-curious reader, systems-minded browser, skeptical visitor who needs proof this fantasy has bones.
- Reader temperature: Curious but cautious.
- Voice posture: Clear, intelligent, and composed.

## Copy requirements

- Headline / hero copy: Signal that the world runs on rules, not decorative sparkles.
- Supporting body copy: Define the systems cleanly in language a smart new visitor can actually absorb.
- Section intros: Each of the three systems needs its own distinct conceptual identity.
- CTA labels: `Explore Pulse Resonance`, `Explore Wordframe`, `Explore Covenant`, `Read the Notes`, `Start with the Book`.
- Microcopy: Any downstream-practice note should stay light and non-pinning.

## Emotional beats

1. Opening beat: The visitor sees that the world is structured.
2. Deepening beat: The three systems reveal different forms of power and consequence.
3. Closing beat: The visitor wants to read the book or continue into a specific system.

## Boundaries

- Must stay suggestive, not explicit, where: System summaries and any downstream-practice framing.
- Must avoid over-explaining: Edge cases, exhaustive rulesets, deprecated frameworks, and fluid canon areas better left mobile.
- Must preserve flexibility in canon around: Downstream practices such as collaring and any future system consequences.
- Must route deeper material to Substack when: A system explanation wants essay length or evolving interpretive detail.

## Canon anchors

- Facts that must remain true: The three foundational top-level systems are `Pulse Resonance`, `Wordframe`, and `Covenant`.
- Character or world references allowed: The House, ritual architecture, desire, cosmological consequence, downstream practices in restrained form.
- References to avoid: `Mirror`, `Constellation Bonding`, explicit demonstration copy, future-canon overlock.

## Social / channel integration

- Social prompts or labels needed: None beyond support.
- Substack bridge language needed: Present it as where deeper notes and extended thinking live.
- Retailer bridge language needed: The book should remain the cleanest proof that these systems work in motion.

## Deliverables

- Required copy blocks: Collection intro, three system summaries, optional downstream-practice note, CTA labels.
- Optional alternates: One more lyrical intro and one more clinical-clear intro.
- Open questions for Lead Elly: Should `How Collaring Works` be named on the collection page immediately or held back until its downstream definition is more stable?
