# The House Page Contract

## Identity

- Page name: The House
- Slug / route: `/house/`
- Page family: Threshold
- Status: Seeded v1

## Page job

- Primary purpose: Fulfill the homepage promise of entering the wider Pendaleverse through the House as a living structure, not just through the book or the systems in isolation.
- Secondary purpose: Orient the reader to the House as the world-entry hub that connects characters, magical systems, and future chambers without forcing every downstream page to debut at once.
- Conversion target: Push visitors deeper into `Characters`, `Magic Systems`, and future room or ritual pages while keeping the book path available as a stabilizing return route.
- Success signal: A visitor can tell what the House is, why it matters, and which deeper door they want to open next without feeling like they were dropped into a mausoleum of unlabeled lore.

## Audience

- Primary audience: Readers who were intrigued by the homepage world path and want to step deeper into the Pendaleverse without immediately buying the book or reading a systems explainer.
- Entry state: Curious, slightly more committed than a homepage visitor, and actively looking for a sense of structure.
- What they probably already know: The House matters, the world has rules, and `When Stars Align` is the current way in.
- What they do not know yet: How the House functions as a connective structure, how people and systems live inside it, and what kind of exploration this site will eventually support.

## Emotional job

- Desired emotional effect: Orientation, implication, desire, and the feeling of crossing from threshold into interior.
- Tension to create: The House should feel alive enough to notice the reader and structured enough to be trusted.
- Relief or reward to provide: A clear map of the next doors without flattening the House into a spreadsheet in evening wear.
- What the page must never feel like: A room directory with no atmosphere, a fandom wiki pretending to be seduction, or a fake-complete lore atlas for pages we have not earned yet.

## Content boundaries

- Must include: House framing, a clear explanation that the House is both place and organizing intelligence, route-out to `Characters` and `Magic Systems`, a future-facing gesture toward rooms or chambers, and a clean return path to the book.
- Must imply: The House has internal logic, selective warmth, ritual intelligence, and downstream spaces still to come.
- Must not include: Explicit scene material, room-by-room canon over-definition, every downstream ritual consequence, or fake-finished architecture for spaces not yet designed.
- Must stay offsite: Deep ritual essays, explicit House material, evolving edge-case canon, and anything better handled in Substack notes while the cosmology is still breathing.

## Narrative and brand rules

- Brand posture: Observant, intimate, elegant, controlled, and faintly predatory in the best way.
- Voice notes: The House should speak through architecture, witness, invitation, and consequence. It should not sound like a haunted-tour pamphlet or a luxury-real-estate brochure with a corruption kink.
- Explicitness boundary: Charged and suggestive only. The House can feel dangerous and warm; it cannot perform explicit material on the public page.
- Lore boundary: This page introduces the House as the world-entry vessel. It does not attempt to explain every chamber, ritual, or emergent practice.

## Information architecture role

- Upstream entry points: Home CTA `Enter the House`, book pages, future social and journal links, search.
- Downstream destinations: `Characters`, `Magic Systems`, future `house` subpages or chambers, future `Journal` notes, and `When Stars Align` as the entry-book anchor.
- Related pages: Home, `Books`, `When Stars Align`, `Characters`, `Magic Systems`, future room or ritual pages.

## CTA logic

- Primary CTA: Enter the systems
- Secondary CTA: Meet the people
- Tertiary CTA: Start with `When Stars Align`

## Social and channel routing

- Social presence on this page: Light support only. The House page should feel like interior architecture, not a social-media hallway.
- Substack role on this page: Present as the place for deeper House notes, room essays, and evolving edges that should remain flexible.
- Amazon or retailer role on this page: Present only as a return path through the book, not as the main point of the page.

## Asset and content needs

- Required copy blocks: Hero, House-definition block, two or three route modules, optional future-chambers teaser, closing CTA rail.
- Required media: At least one strong atmospheric House visual, optional supporting interior imagery, and visual treatment that distinguishes the House from the more direct book path.
- Optional enhancements: Chamber teaser cards, hover or reveal state for route modules, symbolic wayfinding language, a future map-like module that does not over-promise nonexistent pages.

## SEO and metadata intent

- Working title: The House | House Pendale
- Working meta description: Step inside House Pendale and follow the people, systems, and rooms that give the Pendaleverse its structure.
- Structured data notes: `CollectionPage` is the cleanest starting point; individual future room pages can become richer objects later if needed.

## Tech handoff notes

- Template type: Threshold / bridge page that sits between homepage implication and collection-page specificity.
- Reusable components likely needed: Hero, route-module grid or split sections, CTA rail, atmospheric media block, future chamber teaser, footer and shared socials.
- Future expansion hooks: Room pages, ritual pages, glossary edges, `How Collaring Works`, future House journal notes, optional featured chamber on the homepage.

## Creative handoff notes

- Core promise: The House is not just backdrop. It is the living structure through which the Pendaleverse organizes desire, discipline, witness, and consequence.
- Key beats: Arrival -> House definition -> choose your next interior door -> return route for readers who need the book first.
- Phrases or imagery to lean toward: threshold crossed, interior, chambers, grammar, witness, warmth with rules, architecture that notices, doors that lead deeper on purpose.
- Phrases or imagery to avoid: spooky-house camp, generic gothic wallpaper language, haunted-mansion tourism, over-locked canon claims about rooms we have not built yet.
