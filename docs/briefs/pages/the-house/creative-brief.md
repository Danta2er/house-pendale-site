# The House Creative Brief

## Page

- Page name: The House
- Based on contract: `docs/briefs/pages/the-house/page-contract.md`

## Creative objective

- What this page needs to make the reader feel: That the House is a real interior presence with structure, appetite, and intelligence, and that stepping deeper is both safe enough to continue and dangerous enough to matter.
- What this page needs the reader to do next: Choose a meaningful next interior path - `Magic Systems`, `Characters`, or `When Stars Align` - without feeling shoved or over-educated.

## Audience and posture

- Reader type: World-curious visitor, atmosphere-first reader, cautious lurker who wants a stronger sense of the House before picking a person or system.
- Reader temperature: Warmer than the homepage visitor, but still orienting.
- Voice posture: Intimate, precise, architectural, and quietly knowing.

## Copy requirements

- Headline / hero copy: Present the House as a living interior reality, not a decorative setting.
- Supporting body copy: Define the House clearly enough that the reader understands its role, but never so clinically that the heat dies in your hands.
- Section intros: One section should route toward the people, one toward the systems, and one toward future deeper House material.
- CTA labels: Keep them short, directional, and interior-facing. `Meet the People`, `Enter the Systems`, `Start with the Book`, `Read the Notes`.
- Microcopy: Future chambers or room language should imply expansion without pretending we already built the whole manor.

## Emotional beats

1. Opening beat: The reader realizes the House is not scenery. It is an active frame that changes what happens inside it.
2. Deepening beat: The page offers the reader a choice of interior pathways rather than a single lecture track.
3. Closing beat: The reader leaves with a felt sense of the House and a decisive next door to open.

## Boundaries

- Must stay suggestive, not explicit, where: Hero copy, House description, route modules, and closing invitation.
- Must avoid over-explaining: Room taxonomy, ritual specifics, deep cosmological mechanics, and any rigid spatial canon that may still evolve.
- Must preserve flexibility in canon around: Future rooms, interior practices, any secondary House routes, and evolving language around downstream consequence.
- Must route deeper material to Substack when: The copy wants to become essayistic, intimate, or overly specific about House lore not yet stabilized for the site.

## Canon anchors

- Facts that must remain true: The House is central, structured, alive in effect, and meaningfully connected to both the people and the systems of the Pendaleverse.
- Character or world references allowed: Elysia, Coren, the cast collectively, `Pulse Resonance`, `Wordframe`, `Covenant`, ritual architecture, witness, consequence.
- References to avoid: `Mirror` as a live system, `Constellation Bonding`, `Star in Harlem`, explicit House material, and over-defined room canon.

## Social / channel integration

- Social prompts or labels needed: Minimal if any. This page should not feel like it stopped to wave at Instagram in the middle of a ritual corridor.
- Substack bridge language needed: Frame Substack as where deeper House notes, chamber essays, and evolving edges can live with more privacy and elasticity.
- Retailer bridge language needed: The book is the cleanest stabilizing route back out if the reader wants story first.

## Deliverables

- Required copy blocks: Hero, House definition paragraph, people-path module, systems-path module, future chambers / deeper-notes module, CTA labels, close.
- Optional alternates: Two hero variants - one more architectural, one more intimate.
- Open questions for Lead Elly: Do we want the first version of this page to gesture toward specific future rooms, or keep the room concept abstract until those pages are contracted?
