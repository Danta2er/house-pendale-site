# Home Creative Brief

## Page

- Page name: Home
- Based on contract: `docs/briefs/pages/home/page-contract.md`

## Creative objective

- What this page needs to make the reader feel: That they have found a world with intelligence, appetite, and rules, and that stepping further in will reward them.
- What this page needs the reader to do next: Choose one of two clean paths - start with `When Stars Align`, or enter the wider House.

## Audience and posture

- Reader type: New visitor, romantasy browser, genre-aware lurker, social click-through visitor.
- Reader temperature: Interested but uncommitted.
- Voice posture: Knowing, composed, intimate, and precise.

## Copy requirements

- Headline / hero copy: Frame the House as a threshold, not a generic brand banner.
- Supporting body copy: Establish the world as cosmological, sensual, and structured without dumping lore in the reader's lap.
- Section intros: One section should privilege the book path; one should privilege the world path.
- CTA labels: Keep them short, decisive, and legible. `Read When Stars Align`, `Enter the House`, `Follow the House`, `Read the Notes`.
- Microcopy: Social / join language should feel like invitation, not newsletter spam in a cocktail dress.

## Emotional beats

1. Opening beat: The reader arrives at the threshold and senses that the House is already awake.
2. Deepening beat: The page offers two forms of temptation - the book and the world behind it.
3. Closing beat: The reader leaves with a clear next step and a sense that there is more waiting elsewhere.

## Boundaries

- Must stay suggestive, not explicit, where: Hero copy, House framing, book pitch, and social close.
- Must avoid over-explaining: Full constellation mechanics, character webs, house-room lore, or the entire magic system.
- Must preserve flexibility in canon around: Relationship structures and any evolving downstream ritual terminology.
- Must route deeper material to Substack when: The copy wants to drift into essay, canon commentary, or intimate expansion.

## Canon anchors

- Facts that must remain true: `When Stars Align` is the current entry book; the House matters; the magic has rules; the site is pre-explicit on the surface.
- Character or world references allowed: Elysia, Coren, the House, ritual architecture, cosmological stakes, aligned longing.
- References to avoid: `Star in Harlem`, scene-specific explicit beats, deprecated magic terminology, over-locked future canon.

## Social / channel integration

- Social prompts or labels needed: `Follow the House` or equivalent framing for active socials.
- Substack bridge language needed: Present Substack as the place for deeper notes, world edges, and more intimate expansion.
- Retailer bridge language needed: The book path should feel like the first ritual door, not a blunt storefront shove.

## Deliverables

- Required copy blocks: Hero, supporting threshold paragraph, current-book block, world-entry block, social / Substack close, CTA labels.
- Optional alternates: Two or three hero variants with different degrees of severity versus warmth.
- Open questions for Lead Elly: How much overt mention of Liora as author-brand belongs on the homepage versus letting the House lead?
