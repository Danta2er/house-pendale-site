# Home Page Contract

## Identity

- Page name: Home
- Slug / route: `/`
- Page family: Threshold
- Status: Seeded v1

## Page job

- Primary purpose: Act as the public threshold into House Pendale and the Pendaleverse.
- Secondary purpose: Sell `When Stars Align` without flattening the site into a plain book ad.
- Conversion target: Push readers toward either the current book page or deeper world entry.
- Success signal: A first-time visitor can tell what kind of world this is, what book to start with, and where to go next within one screen and one scroll.

## Audience

- Primary audience: Romantasy and erotic-urban-fantasy readers encountering the brand for the first time.
- Entry state: Curious, cautious, and looking for either a strong book hook or a signal that this world is worth entering.
- What they probably already know: Very little beyond genre cues, if anything.
- What they do not know yet: The rules of the House, the emotional architecture of the world, and why this site is not just another author brochure.

## Emotional job

- Desired emotional effect: Implication, hunger, curiosity, and a sense that the reader has crossed a threshold.
- Tension to create: The feeling that the House already knows something about them before they know anything about it.
- Relief or reward to provide: Clear pathways into the book, the world, and the author's living channels.
- What the page must never feel like: A generic author homepage, a fandom wiki, or a lore lecture in a corset.

## Content boundaries

- Must include: Current-book hook, world-entry cue, House framing, clean navigation, social and Substack presence, clear CTA paths.
- Must imply: The House is active, the world is structured, desire and cosmology are entangled, and the reader is welcome but not fully informed yet.
- Must not include: Explicit scene detail, exhaustive lore, every character, or every rule of the magic system.
- Must stay offsite: Explicit expansions, intimate deep dives, and anything better handled as Substack ritual material.

## Narrative and brand rules

- Brand posture: Controlled, intimate, elegant, dangerous, knowing.
- Voice notes: Serious without self-importance, sensual without purple prose, intelligent without academic sludge.
- Explicitness boundary: Charged and suggestive only. The public homepage can promise heat; it should not perform it.
- Lore boundary: Tease system and world logic without hard-defining every downstream consequence.

## Information architecture role

- Upstream entry points: Direct URL, search result, social bio links, Substack links, shared campaign links.
- Downstream destinations: `Books`, `When Stars Align`, `The House`, `Characters`, `Magic Systems`, `Join`.
- Related pages: `About`, `Contact`, future `Journal`.

## CTA logic

- Primary CTA: Read `When Stars Align`
- Secondary CTA: Enter the House
- Tertiary CTA: Join the House / Follow the House

## Social and channel routing

- Social presence on this page: Visible but not screaming. Use a `Follow the House` cluster in the drawer or near the lower page close, plus the full set in the footer.
- Substack role on this page: Present as the place for deeper notes, rituals, and world expansion.
- Amazon or retailer role on this page: Available through the book path, not allowed to dominate the entire threshold experience.

## Asset and content needs

- Required copy blocks: Hero, current-book hook, world-entry block, optional character tease, social / join close, CTA labels.
- Required media: Strong hero image or motion-capable visual, `When Stars Align` cover, House-related atmospheric imagery.
- Optional enhancements: Modal excerpt, layered reveal transitions, animated drawer state, teaser cards for deeper areas.

## SEO and metadata intent

- Working title: House Pendale | Enter the House
- Working meta description: Enter House Pendale, where desire, ritual, and cosmology collide. Start with When Stars Align or step deeper into the world.
- Structured data notes: `WebSite` and `Organization` or `Person` schema, plus clean Open Graph and social metadata.

## Tech handoff notes

- Template type: Threshold template with reusable hero, route block, and channel-close modules.
- Reusable components likely needed: Header / drawer nav, hero, CTA pair, feature cards, social cluster, footer.
- Future expansion hooks: Journal teaser, latest release module, rotating featured character or room, gated-content bridge if one is ever added.

## Creative handoff notes

- Core promise: This is not just a place to buy a book. It is the front door to a living world, and the current book is the cleanest way inside it.
- Key beats: Threshold -> book temptation -> world invitation -> social / Substack continuation.
- Phrases or imagery to lean toward: threshold, House, invitation, witness, doors, alignment, discipline, warmth inside danger.
- Phrases or imagery to avoid: destiny cliches, generic dark-romance boilerplate, pseudo-aristocratic fluff, explicit dirty talk on the homepage.
