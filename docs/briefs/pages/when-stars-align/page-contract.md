# When Stars Align Page Contract

## Identity

- Page name: When Stars Align
- Slug / route: `/books/when-stars-align/`
- Page family: Chamber
- Status: Seeded v1

## Page job

- Primary purpose: Sell `When Stars Align` as the first reading threshold into the Pendaleverse.
- Secondary purpose: Establish the emotional and cosmological contracts of the book without replacing the reading experience.
- Conversion target: Move the visitor to a buy or read-now action with enough confidence and hunger to commit.
- Success signal: A visitor can understand the premise, tone, heat profile, and entry-point value of the book, then choose to buy or read more.

## Audience

- Primary audience: Readers deciding whether this specific book is for them.
- Entry state: Interested, evaluating tone and content, often comparing this book against other genre options.
- What they probably already know: The title, a bit of visual atmosphere, or a hook from the homepage or social.
- What they do not know yet: The premise, emotional arc, stakes, and why this book matters as the first door.

## Emotional job

- Desired emotional effect: Desire, trust, curiosity, and the sense that this book will deliver both heat and structure.
- Tension to create: The visitor should feel that Elysia's first crack is also their own first step into the House.
- Relief or reward to provide: Clean understanding of what kind of book this is and who it is for.
- What the page must never feel like: A spoiler sheet, a KDP metadata dump, or a content-warning bunker with a candle in it.

## Content boundaries

- Must include: Core hook, concise premise, heat and content positioning, emotional promise, buy path, optional path to deeper world notes.
- Must imply: The House matters, the magic has rules, and this is a deliberate beginning.
- Must not include: Full scene summaries, explicit excerpts on the public page, or lore so dense it becomes homework.
- Must stay offsite: Explicit expansions, intimate essays, and any deep-dive commentary better suited to Substack.

## Narrative and brand rules

- Brand posture: Authoritative, seductive, and emotionally literate.
- Voice notes: Promise the book honestly. Do not over-inflate or make it sound safer than it is.
- Explicitness boundary: High-heat positioning is allowed; explicit textual performance is not.
- Lore boundary: Explain enough to intrigue, not enough to collapse the wonder.

## Information architecture role

- Upstream entry points: Home, Books collection, search results, social campaigns, direct links.
- Downstream destinations: Amazon or retailer links, `Characters`, `Magic Systems`, `Join`, optional Substack companion material.
- Related pages: Books, Home, future series pages.

## CTA logic

- Primary CTA: Buy or read `When Stars Align`
- Secondary CTA: Meet the characters
- Tertiary CTA: Read deeper notes on Substack

## Social and channel routing

- Social presence on this page: Minimal support only.
- Substack role on this page: Companion-depth bridge for readers who want essays, notes, and expansion without overloading the sales page.
- Amazon or retailer role on this page: Central and unambiguous.

## Asset and content needs

- Required copy blocks: Hook, premise, reading-experience or selling-beats section, content and heat note, CTA block.
- Required media: Cover image, optional supporting artwork or detail imagery.
- Optional enhancements: Quote rail, excerpt trigger if ever gated or carefully curated, format badges.

## SEO and metadata intent

- Working title: When Stars Align | House Pendale
- Working meta description: Enter House Pendale through When Stars Align, an explicit urban fantasy of ritual, desire, and cosmological consequence.
- Structured data notes: `Book` schema with author, image, description, and retailer linkage.

## Tech handoff notes

- Template type: Chamber template for an individual book.
- Reusable components likely needed: Book hero, premise block, selling-beats module, content-note module, retailer CTA rail.
- Future expansion hooks: Reviews, alternate editions, excerpt handling, media embeds, series navigation.

## Creative handoff notes

- Core promise: This is the book where the House opens and the reader stops pretending they were not curious.
- Key beats: Hook -> premise -> emotional and heat promise -> confidence to buy -> optional deeper-world bridge.
- Phrases or imagery to lean toward: crack, threshold, House, alignment, disciplined desire, consequence, arrival.
- Phrases or imagery to avoid: generic romantasy mush, false coyness about heat, spoiler-heavy play-by-play.
