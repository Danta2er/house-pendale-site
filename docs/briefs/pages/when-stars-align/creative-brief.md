# When Stars Align Creative Brief

## Page

- Page name: When Stars Align
- Based on contract: `docs/briefs/pages/when-stars-align/page-contract.md`

## Creative objective

- What this page needs to make the reader feel: That this book is intelligent, filthy, emotionally structured, and exactly the right first step for the kind of reader it is calling.
- What this page needs the reader to do next: Buy the book, or at minimum move one click closer to buying it.

## Audience and posture

- Reader type: Prospective buyer, genre comparison shopper, social-referral clicker, curious lurker deciding whether to trust the heat level.
- Reader temperature: Warm but still deciding.
- Voice posture: Precise, seductive, and honest.

## Copy requirements

- Headline / hero copy: Lead with a hook strong enough to stop the scroll and frame the premise.
- Supporting body copy: Convey the premise, stakes, and emotional architecture without sounding like jacket-copy soup.
- Section intros: One section should define the reading experience; another should clarify content and heat honestly.
- CTA labels: `Read When Stars Align`, `Buy the Book`, `Meet the Characters`, `Read the Notes`.
- Microcopy: Any content note or format text should be calm, clean, and matter-of-fact.

## Emotional beats

1. Opening beat: The reader meets the crack in Elysia's life and wants to follow it.
2. Deepening beat: The page promises that heat, discipline, humor, and cosmological stakes belong to the same architecture.
3. Closing beat: The book feels inevitable enough to buy.

## Boundaries

- Must stay suggestive, not explicit, where: Hook copy, reading-experience sections, and any descriptive promise.
- Must avoid over-explaining: Full world rules, the full constellation, spoiler turns, and chapter-level beats.
- Must preserve flexibility in canon around: Future-series emphasis and downstream world-expansion details.
- Must route deeper material to Substack when: The page begins drifting into essay, ritual analysis, or intimate expansion.

## Canon anchors

- Facts that must remain true: Elysia is the entry protagonist; Coren has been waiting a very long time; the House matters; the magic has rules; the book is explicit erotic urban fantasy.
- Character or world references allowed: Elysia, Coren, the House, Durham, ritual architecture, alignment, cosmological consequence.
- References to avoid: `Star in Harlem`, future-book spoilers, deprecated magic language, explicit scene reenactment.

## Social / channel integration

- Social prompts or labels needed: None beyond support.
- Substack bridge language needed: Frame it as notes, companion thought, and deeper world edges for those already leaning in.
- Retailer bridge language needed: Make the buy path feel direct and inevitable, not cheesy or over-caffeinated.

## Deliverables

- Required copy blocks: Hero hook, premise block, reading-experience block, content and heat note, CTA labels, optional companion-note bridge.
- Optional alternates: One hero variant that leans harder into Elysia and one that leans harder into the House.
- Open questions for Lead Elly: How prominently should the content/heat positioning appear above the fold versus lower on the page?
