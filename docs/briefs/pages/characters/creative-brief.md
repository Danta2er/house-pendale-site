# Characters Creative Brief

## Page

- Page name: Characters
- Based on contract: `docs/briefs/pages/characters/page-contract.md`

## Creative objective

- What this page needs to make the reader feel: That each of these people matters, and that choosing whom to follow next is part of the seduction.
- What this page needs the reader to do next: Click into a character page or return to the book with stronger attachment.

## Audience and posture

- Reader type: Character-driven reader, attachment-first browser, existing visitor seeking a face or name to hold onto.
- Reader temperature: Curious and emotionally available.
- Voice posture: Intimate, perceptive, and selective.

## Copy requirements

- Headline / hero copy: Frame the cast as presences that alter the atmosphere of the House.
- Supporting body copy: Give readers a way into each person without flattening them into trivia cards.
- Section intros: Each character card or block needs a distinct emotional hook.
- CTA labels: `Meet Elysia`, `Meet Coren`, `Meet Hannah`, `Meet Rachel`, `Meet Ezra`, `Start with When Stars Align`.
- Microcopy: Any relationship or role labels should stay clean and non-wiki-like.

## Emotional beats

1. Opening beat: The cast page tells the reader these are the people worth watching.
2. Deepening beat: Individual hooks create attachment and curiosity.
3. Closing beat: The reader chooses a person or returns to the book carrying more feeling.

## Boundaries

- Must stay suggestive, not explicit, where: Character hooks and relational framing.
- Must avoid over-explaining: Full dynamics, emotional spoilers, relationship taxonomies, lore jargon.
- Must preserve flexibility in canon around: Evolving roles, future books, and deeper relational revelation.
- Must route deeper material to Substack when: Interior essays or charged character commentary wants more room than the collection page should hold.

## Canon anchors

- Facts that must remain true: The main public cast starts with Elysia, Coren, Hannah, Rachel, and Ezra.
- Character or world references allowed: The House as context, but not as a character card masquerading as one.
- References to avoid: Liora as a cast entry on this page, deprecated terminology, explicit-scene shorthand, overpinned future dynamics.

## Social / channel integration

- Social prompts or labels needed: None beyond support.
- Substack bridge language needed: Present as deeper character notes or essays for readers who want more.
- Retailer bridge language needed: Related-book CTA should feel like the obvious next ritual step.

## Deliverables

- Required copy blocks: Collection intro, five character hooks, related-book bridge, CTA labels.
- Optional alternates: One softer intro and one sharper intro.
- Open questions for Lead Elly: Should Coren and Elysia always be visually privileged first, or should the page be allowed to spotlight others as the world expands?
