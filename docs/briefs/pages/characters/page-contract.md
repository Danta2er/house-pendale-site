# Characters Page Contract

## Identity

- Page name: Characters
- Slug / route: `/characters/`
- Page family: Collection
- Status: Seeded v1

## Page job

- Primary purpose: Introduce the people at the heart of the Pendaleverse and route readers into individual character pages.
- Secondary purpose: Build attachment without forcing the page to carry the entire relationship architecture of the series.
- Conversion target: Push readers toward character detail pages and back toward the books.
- Success signal: A visitor can understand who matters, why they matter, and which person they want to follow next.

## Audience

- Primary audience: Readers who attach through people first and want an emotional map of the world.
- Entry state: Curious about the cast, often arriving after the homepage or a book page.
- What they probably already know: A name or image, maybe Elysia or Coren from a hook.
- What they do not know yet: The broader constellation of presences and how to navigate them.

## Emotional job

- Desired emotional effect: Attraction, curiosity, and personal attachment.
- Tension to create: The feeling that every person on the page changes the shape of the House.
- Relief or reward to provide: A clean, navigable cast map without drowning the visitor in relationship algebra.
- What the page must never feel like: A mood board pretending to be character architecture, a spoiler chart, or a fandom wiki with a silk ribbon tied around it.

## Content boundaries

- Must include: Elysia, Coren, Hannah, Rachel, and Ezra as actual character-entry points.
- Must imply: The cast is relational, structured, and emotionally consequential.
- Must not include: The House and Liora as if they are interchangeable with cast entries on the main character grid.
- Must stay offsite: Deep character essays, explicit emotional dissections, and material better handled as Substack deep dives.

## Narrative and brand rules

- Brand posture: Intimate, selective, and observant.
- Voice notes: Character presentation should feel like an invitation to approach, not a dossier from a police procedural.
- Explicitness boundary: Charged and suggestive only.
- Lore boundary: Enough to orient and entice; not enough to flatten every relationship into summary.

## Information architecture role

- Upstream entry points: Home, books, future journal posts, search.
- Downstream destinations: Individual character pages, related books, future House or system pages where appropriate.
- Related pages: Home, Books, `When Stars Align`, future character details.

## CTA logic

- Primary CTA: Meet the cast
- Secondary CTA: Start with `When Stars Align`
- Tertiary CTA: Read deeper notes on Substack

## Social and channel routing

- Social presence on this page: Background support only.
- Substack role on this page: Optional deep-character bridge for readers who want more interiority and commentary.
- Amazon or retailer role on this page: Secondary support through related book links.

## Asset and content needs

- Required copy blocks: Character-collection intro, individual entry hooks, related-book bridge.
- Required media: Distinct character imagery for Elysia, Coren, Hannah, Rachel, and Ezra.
- Optional enhancements: Relationship hints, featured character spotlight, grouped cards for current-book relevance.

## SEO and metadata intent

- Working title: Characters | House Pendale
- Working meta description: Meet the characters who shape House Pendale, beginning with the people at the heart of When Stars Align.
- Structured data notes: `CollectionPage`; individual character pages may later warrant structured person-style metadata where useful, but not in a way that gets goofy.

## Tech handoff notes

- Template type: Collection template with scalable character cards and detail-page routing.
- Reusable components likely needed: Character hero, card grid, featured character row, related-book bridge.
- Future expansion hooks: More character cards, filters by book or house role, quote snippets, related-world modules.

## Creative handoff notes

- Core promise: These are not archetypes in costume. These are the people who make the House dangerous, tender, funny, and impossible to leave.
- Key beats: Orientation -> cast curiosity -> individual attachment -> route back to books or deeper notes.
- Phrases or imagery to lean toward: presence, approach, orbit, pull, seat at the table, someone changing the air in a room.
- Phrases or imagery to avoid: cast list stiffness, fandom shipping jargon, faux-mystery vagueness that says nothing.
