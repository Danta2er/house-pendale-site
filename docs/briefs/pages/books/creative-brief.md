# Books Creative Brief

## Page

- Page name: Books
- Based on contract: `docs/briefs/pages/books/page-contract.md`

## Creative objective

- What this page needs to make the reader feel: That entering the books is simple, deliberate, and rewarding.
- What this page needs the reader to do next: Click into `When Stars Align` without wondering whether they are starting in the wrong place.

## Audience and posture

- Reader type: New reader, casual browser, social-referral clicker, future repeat visitor checking the shelf.
- Reader temperature: Interested and evaluating commitment.
- Voice posture: Clear, inviting, intelligent, and controlled.

## Copy requirements

- Headline / hero copy: Establish that there is a current entry point and that beginning matters.
- Supporting body copy: Offer reading confidence without sounding like a syllabus.
- Section intros: Feature the current book first, with language that leaves elegant space for later titles.
- CTA labels: `Start with When Stars Align`, `Enter the House`, `Read the Notes`.
- Microcopy: Release-state or reading-order labels should be simple and unsnobbish.

## Emotional beats

1. Opening beat: The page tells the reader there is a clear beginning.
2. Deepening beat: The featured book feels like a chosen threshold, not a commodity tile.
3. Closing beat: The reader leaves with momentum toward the first book and awareness that more waits beyond it.

## Boundaries

- Must stay suggestive, not explicit, where: Collection intro, featured summaries, and any future-series framing.
- Must avoid over-explaining: Book-by-book lore breakdowns, world mechanics, or emotional spoilage.
- Must preserve flexibility in canon around: Future titles, release order, and evolving series shape.
- Must route deeper material to Substack when: Companion reading, author notes, or deeper thematic commentary begins to crowd the shelf.

## Canon anchors

- Facts that must remain true: `When Stars Align` is the current entry title and the cleanest starting point.
- Character or world references allowed: Elysia, Coren, the House, the idea of alignment, the sense of a world opening through the books.
- References to avoid: Explicit scene beats, over-specific future-book claims, deprecated system terminology.

## Social / channel integration

- Social prompts or labels needed: None beyond light supporting presence.
- Substack bridge language needed: Present it as companion notes, deeper readings, and world edges.
- Retailer bridge language needed: Keep the book path ceremonial and enticing, not like a checkout aisle.

## Deliverables

- Required copy blocks: Collection intro, current featured book hook, reading-path note, future-expansion placeholder language, CTA labels.
- Optional alternates: One warmer variant and one sharper variant for the intro.
- Open questions for Lead Elly: How overtly should future unpublished titles be named on the live collection page before release timing is ready?
