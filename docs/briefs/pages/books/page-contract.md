# Books Page Contract

## Identity

- Page name: Books
- Slug / route: `/books/`
- Page family: Collection
- Status: Seeded v1

## Page job

- Primary purpose: Present the Pendaleverse reading path through books, starting with the current entry point and leaving room for a growing catalog.
- Secondary purpose: Reassure new readers that there is a clear place to start even as the world expands.
- Conversion target: Push visitors toward the `When Stars Align` detail page and then onward to retailer purchase.
- Success signal: A visitor can understand where to begin, what kind of reading experience is being offered, and how future books will fit without the page collapsing into clutter.

## Audience

- Primary audience: New or lightly informed readers who want the cleanest route into the series.
- Entry state: Interested, curious about reading order, and deciding whether to commit.
- What they probably already know: At most a title, a vibe, or a social recommendation.
- What they do not know yet: How the books relate to one another, whether the series is accessible, and whether they need a lore decoder ring to start.

## Emotional job

- Desired emotional effect: Confidence, invitation, and the sense that each book is a deliberate door into the same living structure.
- Tension to create: The reader should feel that beginning here matters.
- Relief or reward to provide: Absolute clarity on where to start and why.
- What the page must never feel like: A cold product grid, an Amazon mirror, or a timeline spreadsheet wearing perfume.

## Content boundaries

- Must include: Featured current entry book, clean routing to individual book pages, room for future books, brief reading-path framing, retailer bridge logic.
- Must imply: The series has shape and intention; the world can grow without becoming chaos.
- Must not include: Full spoilers, detailed lore essays, explicit scene description, or a fake-complete bookshelf when only one published entry is live.
- Must stay offsite: Intimate essays, explicit expansions, and deeper canon commentary better suited to Substack.

## Narrative and brand rules

- Brand posture: Curated, confident, and seductive without sounding like a sales department.
- Voice notes: This page should present the books as doors, not inventory units.
- Explicitness boundary: Promise intensity and heat without performing explicit material.
- Lore boundary: Enough world context to orient a reader; not enough to replace the books.

## Information architecture role

- Upstream entry points: Home, social links, search results for books or author queries, future campaign links.
- Downstream destinations: `When Stars Align`, future book pages, `Characters`, `Magic Systems`, `Join`.
- Related pages: Home, future series pages, future reading-order support if needed.

## CTA logic

- Primary CTA: Start with `When Stars Align`
- Secondary CTA: Explore the world
- Tertiary CTA: Join for notes and release updates

## Social and channel routing

- Social presence on this page: Footer and light supporting presence only. Do not let socials overpower the reading path.
- Substack role on this page: Frame Substack as where deeper notes and companion material live.
- Amazon or retailer role on this page: Retailer links should exist per book card or detail page, not overwhelm the collection view.

## Asset and content needs

- Required copy blocks: Books intro, reading-path note, featured-book card content, future-series framing.
- Required media: Book covers and supporting atmospheric imagery only where it clarifies hierarchy.
- Optional enhancements: Reading-order badge, release-state tag, teaser modules for upcoming titles.

## SEO and metadata intent

- Working title: Books | House Pendale
- Working meta description: Begin the Pendaleverse with When Stars Align and follow the books as the House expands.
- Structured data notes: `CollectionPage` plus `ItemList`; individual books should support `Book` schema on their own pages.

## Tech handoff notes

- Template type: Collection template with featured item support and scalable card rows.
- Reusable components likely needed: Collection hero, featured-book module, book cards, release-state badge, CTA rails.
- Future expansion hooks: Multiple series, reading-order views, preorder modules, signed-book or direct-sale paths if later needed.

## Creative handoff notes

- Core promise: There is a right place to begin, and it is meant to pull you further in.
- Key beats: Orientation -> current entry point -> sense of future expansion -> invitation onward.
- Phrases or imagery to lean toward: doors, thresholds, first step, first alignment, shelves that feel curated rather than stocked.
- Phrases or imagery to avoid: generic romance blurbs, high-fantasy bureaucracy, dead bookstore language.
