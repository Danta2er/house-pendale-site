# Creative Wave 01 Lead Notes

## Status

The draft is strong enough to keep moving.

This is not a restart.

The voice is largely right, the public-site boundary is mostly holding, and the page set feels more like House Pendale than a generic author funnel. What it needs now is a tightening pass so it stops locking future structure too early and trims a few phrases that are more clever than useful.

## Overall verdict

Keep:

- the threshold logic on `Home`
- the confidence and warmth of the `Characters` section
- the general structure of `When Stars Align`
- the clarity and restraint of `Magic Systems`
- the consistent Substack boundary across all five pages

Revise:

- any copy that overcommits future architecture
- any phrase that sounds more impressed with itself than with the reader's clarity
- any public-facing system explanation that pins unstable canon too hard

## Required revision pass

### 1. Home

What is working:

- The hero is strong.
- The threshold tone is correct.
- `Follow the House` is clean and useful.

What needs revision:

- The `Enter the House` promise is still leaning on architecture that does not exist yet.
- References to `rooms already waiting to close around you` are sexy, but they are also a promise. Keep the mood, reduce the implied specificity until the House route is actually seeded.

Revision instruction:

- Keep the threshold mood.
- Slightly soften references to rooms and map-depth so the copy does not outrun the architecture.
- Let the world-entry path feel real without pretending the House section already has fully defined chambers.

### 2. Books

What is working:

- `Every House has a first door` is good.
- The page correctly centers `When Stars Align`.
- The shelf language leaves room for growth.

What needs revision:

- `The Pendaleverse is built to be entered in order` locks us into a stricter reading-order promise than we may want.
- `First in the Constellation Cycle` may be true, but it is still stronger series architecture than we need on the public shelf right now.

Revision instruction:

- Preserve the idea that there is a clean place to begin.
- Remove or soften any language that implies a permanently rigid reading sequence unless Lead Elly explicitly locks that later.
- Favor `start here` over `must be entered in order`.

Suggested direction:

- Instead of `built to be entered in order`, use language closer to:
  - `There is a clear place to begin.`
  - `If you are entering the House for the first time, start here.`

### 3. When Stars Align

What is working:

- The hero copy is strong.
- The premise block is persuasive.
- `Heat with a spine` is a good section anchor.
- The consent / consequence seriousness is coming through clearly.

What needs revision:

- `Multi-partner constellation architecture` is too clinical and too internal-jargon-adjacent for a public sales page.
- A few phrases are brushing against “smart-sounding abstraction” when the page’s real job is to help someone decide to buy the book.

Revision instruction:

- Keep the page intelligent and high-heat.
- Make the content-positioning language slightly more human and less systems-theory flavored.
- Favor reader clarity over internal-world elegance.

Suggested direction:

- Replace `multi-partner constellation architecture` with something more legible, such as:
  - `polyamorous relationship dynamics`
  - `multi-partner relationship dynamics`
  - `an established multi-partner dynamic`

### 4. Characters

What is working:

- This is the strongest correction in the packet.
- The page now behaves like a real cast page instead of a mood collage.
- The five-character focus is right.
- Distinctions between the characters are coming through.

What needs revision:

- Very little structurally.
- Just watch for any line that starts sounding like every character is being described in the same velvet voice.

Revision instruction:

- Keep the structure.
- Sharpen differentiation even more if possible.
- Make sure each character sounds like a distinct pressure in the House, not the same pressure in five different coats.

### 5. Magic Systems

What is working:

- The page understands its job.
- The three-system taxonomy is holding.
- It stays on the right side of the lore boundary.
- The downstream-practice language is smart and restrained.

What needs revision:

- `Pulse Resonance` may be pinned a little too specifically in public-facing language.
- `Craving, breath, surrender, and earned release` is evocative, but it may imply a narrower mechanism than we want to harden here.

Revision instruction:

- Keep the embodied-power framing.
- Leave slightly more interpretive air in the description.
- Do not let the public page become the place where flexible system nuance gets accidentally canonized.

Suggested direction:

- Keep `the body is evidence` if it still feels right.
- Broaden the setup language slightly so it signals embodiment and truth without over-defining the exact mechanism stack.

## Tone notes

The draft is at its best when it is:

- precise
- controlled
- intelligent
- slightly dangerous
- reader-oriented

The draft weakens when it becomes:

- over-declared
- too pleased with its own phrasing
- more architectural than emotionally legible
